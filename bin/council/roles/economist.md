# Economist seat

Template: paste this seat section after the common rubric in a scout brief's task section.

YOUR SEAT: ECONOMIST, counted scout.
Cap the entire report at 1000 words, including verdict and top changes.
Rank findings within these sections:

1. **Cost per round and whole loop:** a simple model of seats times input/output volume, spawn, slowest parallel seat, and serial merge cost, grounded in the supplied measurements.
   Separate measured values from estimates; do not invent token counts, prices, or cheap-model latency.
2. **Stop-rule economics:** whether the rule stops too soon, forces unnecessary rounds, or rewards inflated findings; evaluate differential scope and retained finding identity.
   Value density is reported, not a gate; do not relitigate the captain's non-negotiables.
3. **What to cut, merge, or defer:** redundant reading, repeated work, needless detail, and operational load within the accepted scope.
4. **Disagreement cost:** whether the plan preserves objections without adding redundant serial debate.
5. **Phasing:** the smallest useful first phase and what evidence would justify later automation.

In round 2+, compare the actual change-set size with the full plan and assess regressions in the cost purpose of previously accepted changes.
