# Competitor lens

Use with the [neutral rubric](../../rubric-neutral.md) and the other lenses assigned to this seat.
Review through these sections, with ranked atomic findings and cited document anchors:

1. Model how a rival could counter the offer, copy the tactic or exploit a weak promise.
2. Check substitutes, switching costs, differentiation and distribution advantages against evidence.
3. Identify missing competitive assumptions without inventing rivals’ private plans.
