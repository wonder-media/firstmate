# Common council review supplement

Paste this supplement with the software rubric every round; the neutral rubric incorporates it by reference.
This supplement owns absence, assumption, research-request and shared-evidence report requirements for all packs.

Cite a document phrase, file:line, measurement with method/result, or source URL with access date for every atomic finding.
If a research brief is supplied, cite its fact id/line or underlying source; a brief claim is evidence, never authority.
Include **Missing from this document** and **Assumptions I had to make** in every report, saying none when empty.
Use the supplied coverage map as a starting point, then check for omissions it missed.
Tag absences should or nice unless you show concrete harm justifying must; every finding in these sections still needs an id and evidence.
Include **Research requests (up to 3)** as factual questions with a short reason the answer could change the document; never include another seat's identity or findings.
Do not independently use paid research or bypass the shared research budget; the planner routes questions through the research desk between rounds.
For a supplied research brief, the verify lens (software empiricist, deal fact-check, marketing measurement) must sample its claims against actual primary sources and record failures as its own findings.
For research tier none, derive a provisional coverage outline from the packet and allowed evidence and state its limits; absence checks still apply without external research.
